Setup
Keep Utilizer running.
Enable CS2 console: Settings > Game > Developer Console.
Open the console (~):
  con_logfile console.log
Then join a local practice map.
In Utilizer: Ctrl+S. Back in CS2:
  exec cfg

Capture
Stand and aim, then F6 records the position.
Mode: Captures > zone > double-click > name > Save.
Delete discards a capture.

Add manually
Add spot > name + position > Save.
  setpos X Y Z;setang P Y R

Practice
Mode: Saved > zone > select a spot > Ctrl+S.
In CS2: exec cfg (repeat after every sync).
  ← / →   Previous / next (wraps)
  ↑       Repeat current spot
  ↓       Look down, keep the spot's yaw
  ut_7    Jump to slot 7, without exec
Chat: [number/total] name.
Slots follow the whole saved map list.

Edit
Double-click or Enter opens details.
Click outside or Escape closes.
Save/Delete are local; Ctrl+S writes cfg.cfg.
Saved and Captures show separate sets.
